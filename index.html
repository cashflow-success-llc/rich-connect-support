SUPPORT

Using Rich Connect

Rich Connect requires a compatible VLESS URL or HTTPS subscription URL supplied separately.

  1. Open Settings in Rich Connect.
  2. Select Configuration.
  3. Paste a compatible VLESS URL or HTTPS subscription URL.
  4. Save the configuration and return to the Home screen.
  5. Tap the connection button to start or stop the connection.
Connection speed and availability depend on the selected server, configuration, network provider, and current network conditions.

Contact support

When contacting support, describe the issue and include the Rich Connect version and iOS version. Do not send configuration URLs, access identifiers, or other connection credentials.

PRIVACY POLICY

Rich Connect Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 24, 2026

Overview

Rich Connect is a client for establishing secure network connections using a configuration provided by the user. This policy explains what information is handled by the app and when information may be received by external services involved in providing a connection.

Information stored on your device

Rich Connect stores the configuration URL or connection configuration you provide, connection preferences, locally calculated connection duration, traffic counters, and limited diagnostic status information on your device. This information is used to operate the app and is not used for advertising.

Subscription requests

If you provide an HTTPS subscription URL, Rich Connect contacts that address to retrieve or refresh the connection configuration. Standard network information, including the IP address and technical request metadata, is processed only in real time to fulfill the request. CASHFLOW SUCCESS L.L.C. does not retain this information after the request is completed. Rich Connect does not send the subscription URL to analytics or advertising services.

Connection servers and network traffic

When you start a connection, network traffic is processed through the server identified by your configuration, except for resources routed directly by the app. The server operator and destination services may process information according to their own policies. Rich Connect does not control independently supplied servers or destination services.

Analytics, advertising, and tracking

Rich Connect does not include third-party advertising SDKs, behavioral analytics SDKs, or cross-app tracking functionality. We do not sell personal information.

Diagnostics

Limited connection status and diagnostic messages may be stored locally to help the user understand connection errors. Rich Connect does not automatically upload these diagnostics to us. If you choose to send diagnostic information to support, remove configuration URLs and access credentials first.

Data retention and deletion

Locally stored configuration and app data remain on the device until you reset the configuration, delete the app, or iOS removes the app data. CASHFLOW SUCCESS L.L.C. does not retain IP addresses or request history from subscription requests. Independently supplied connection servers, network providers, and destination services may apply their own privacy and retention practices.

Children

Rich Connect is not designed to collect personal information from children. The app does not provide account registration, social features, or advertising.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when Rich Connect features or data practices change. The current version will remain available on this page with its effective date.
